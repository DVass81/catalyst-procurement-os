# Catalyst Procurement OS

## Executive sales deck — Y-12 founding-partner conversation

### 1. The outcome

**Make purchasing easier to use, easier to control, and easier to defend.**

Catalyst Procurement OS connects requests, approvals, purchase orders, receiving, invoices, vendor risk, spend intelligence, and audit evidence in one operating system.

### 2. The current friction

- Employees avoid long, confusing forms.
- Purchasing rekeys information and chases approvals.
- Available inventory and approved standards are discovered too late.
- Vendor, contract, budget, and risk context live in different places.
- Invoice exceptions require manual investigation.
- Audit preparation becomes a document-collection project.

### 3. The Catalyst difference

Legacy tools digitize transactions. Catalyst helps people make better procurement decisions.

- Natural-language request intake
- Inventory and standards recommendations before spend occurs
- Risk-adjusted sourcing comparisons
- Human-controlled, role-based approvals
- Connected procure-to-pay records
- Three-way match with plain-language exception explanations
- Examiner-ready evidence by transaction

### 4. Featured demonstration

“We are hiring three new loan officers and need laptops, monitors, docking stations, headsets, and office chairs.”

Catalyst structures the request, finds inventory, corrects a standards issue, compares vendors, confirms budget and coding, routes approvals, creates the PO, records receipt, identifies an invoice variance, and preserves the complete evidence trail.

### 5. Measurable value in one request

- **$1,047** in unnecessary monitor spend avoided
- **$320** unapproved freight charge identified before payment
- Four human approvals presented with decision-ready evidence
- One correlated audit trail from request through invoice exception

### 6. Governance by design

- AI recommendations do not approve financial actions.
- Segregation of duties prevents prohibited self-approval.
- Role, threshold, exception, and audit controls remain visible.
- The demo contains fictional data and no member information.
- Production direction includes tenant isolation, SSO, encryption, retention, and immutable audit controls.

### 7. Catalyst Guide Live

The guided demonstration can narrate, caption, pause for questions, answer from the current page and role, and resume from the exact chapter.

The deterministic tour remains available if live AI, the microphone, or the network is unavailable.
