# Founding-partner pilot proposal

## Offer

Y-12 participates in a paid 30-day pilot as a Catalyst founding partner. The pilot fee is $2,500 and is credited toward the $7,500 implementation if Y-12 proceeds. The remaining implementation fee is $5,000. The continuing subscription is $1,200 per month for one tenant with unlimited users and branches.

Custom integrations, major custom development, migration beyond the agreed pilot dataset, and third-party platform fees are scoped separately.

## Pilot objectives

1. Validate the request-to-invoice workflow with purchasing, Finance, IT, AP, receiving, and audit.
2. Confirm role, threshold, segregation-of-duties, exception, and evidence requirements.
3. Measure intake time, approval cycle time, identified savings, invoice exceptions, and audit-package effort.
4. Prioritize the production backlog and integrations.
5. Decide whether to proceed to the nine-week implementation.

## 30-day structure

- Week 1: discovery, workflow mapping, success baselines, and tenant configuration
- Week 2: configured workflow review and controlled user testing
- Week 3: feedback, prioritized changes, measurement, and security review
- Week 4: validation, executive readout, production scope, and proceed/no-proceed decision

## Nine-week implementation direction

- Weeks 1–2: detailed discovery, controls, tenant, identity, and solution design
- Weeks 3–5: procurement workflows, data, priority integrations, and configuration
- Weeks 6–7: testing, security hardening, evidence, and operational readiness
- Week 8: training, migration rehearsal, and launch preparation
- Week 9: controlled production launch and stabilization

## Customer responsibilities

- Name an executive sponsor and operational lead.
- Provide current workflow, role, threshold, and integration requirements.
- Supply only authorized pilot data through an agreed secure method.
- Participate in weekly review and timely acceptance decisions.

## Catalyst responsibilities

- Maintain the pilot environment and agreed configuration.
- Protect pilot data under the executed agreement.
- Document feedback, decisions, control gaps, and production scope.
- Provide weekly status, risk, and measurement updates.
- Keep AI actions human-gated.

## Acceptance

The pilot is successful when both parties agree that the workflow is materially easier, the control design is acceptable, the value measures are credible, and the nine-week production scope is sufficiently defined.
