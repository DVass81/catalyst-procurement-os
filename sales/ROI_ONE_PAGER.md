# Catalyst Procurement OS — value case

## What changes

Catalyst moves procurement control upstream. It finds available inventory, standards conflicts, contract opportunities, budget issues, and invoice exceptions before they become avoidable cost or audit work.

## Pilot measurement framework

| Outcome | Pilot baseline | Pilot measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request effort | Minutes and clicks per request | Median intake time |
| Approval speed | Hours from submission to decision | Median and 90th percentile cycle time |
| Avoided spend | Purchases made despite available inventory | Accepted inventory savings |
| Contract compliance | Addressable spend outside approved contracts | Under-contract spend percentage |
| Invoice leakage | Unapproved price, freight, tax, or quantity | Exceptions found before payment |
| Audit preparation | Time to assemble a transaction package | Minutes to evidence-ready package |

## Demonstrated examples

- $1,047 avoided through an accepted internal inventory allocation.
- $320 freight variance identified through three-way matching.
- Four sequential approvals with visible segregation of duties.
- One complete evidence trail across request, PO, receipt, and invoice.
